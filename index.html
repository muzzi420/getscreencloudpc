Frequently asked questions

What comes in the PDF?

Your personalized stitch chart, a DMC thread color legend with stitch counts per color, and the pattern size in stitches.

Can I use any name or date?

Yes — the generator renders whatever text you type, so any name, wedding date, or birth date works.

What fabric count should I use?

Any count works; higher counts (like 16 or 18) give a smaller finished piece, lower counts (like 11 or 14) give a larger one.
